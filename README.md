I turn business needs into clear, buildable requirements. I started as an Android and front-end engineer, moved into product ownership and product management, and now work as a Senior Business Analyst on enterprise platforms. The engineering background means I write requirements that developers and QA can actually pick up and run with.

### 💼 What I do now

- **Senior Business Analyst**: run analysis sessions with business stakeholders across client projects and produce BRS documents, user stories, data dictionaries, and process flows in Arabic and English.
- **Senior System Analyst / BA**: break business requirements into actionable items for Tech and QA, map each requirement to the current solution, and write UAT test cases and acceptance criteria.
- **Freelance Product Owner**: help teams shape and deliver products.

### 🧭 Where I've been

- **Product Manager & Product Owner**: led a team of product owners, took a SaaS product from concept to MVP, and set up product KPIs.
- **Product Manager (part-time)**: took a booking product from idea to MVP, built its roadmap and customer journey, and set up a customer feedback loop.
- **Software Engineer & Instructor**: taught OOP, Data Structures, and Operating Systems, and built internal web tools.
- **Android Engineer Intern**: built an e-commerce Android app in Kotlin with clean architecture.

### 🛠️ Skills

**Business Analysis:** requirements elicitation, BRS/BRD, user stories, acceptance criteria, data dictionaries, process mapping (BPMN), sequence diagrams, API contracts (REST/JSON), UAT

**Product:** roadmaps, backlog management, feature prioritization, MVP scoping, KPI tracking, market and competitor research, go-to-market

**Agile:** Scrum, sprint planning, squad structuring, burn-down charts, Definition of Done
